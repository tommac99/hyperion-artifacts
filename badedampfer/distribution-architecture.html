Important Architecture Note

Who Collects the Money

This surprises most operators. On the major OTAs, you do not process the payment — they do. Understanding this changes how you think about Stripe's role in the architecture.

OTA-Collected (most channels)

They Charge.
They Remit.

GetYourGuide, Viator, Booking.com, and Expedia collect payment from the customer. They hold funds and remit to you monthly — minus their commission.

Stripe is not involved in these transactions. You receive bank transfers from the OTA. This means cash flow is delayed by 30-45 days on those bookings.

Accounting implication: you need to track OTA receivables as a separate line. Medusa records the booking; the OTA manages the payment lifecycle.

Operator-Collected (direct + vouchers)

You Charge.
Stripe Handles It.

For direct bookings on your website, Stripe processes payment at checkout. This is where Stripe Connect matters — franchise locations have their own Stripe accounts, and the platform fee splits automatically at payment time.

Jochen Schweizer / mydays vouchers are redeemed on your checkout. The voucher is pre-paid by the gift buyer; you receive the redemption value minus JSMD's commission.

Immediate cash flow. Full control of the customer relationship.

This is not a weekend project. But it is also not three years. The architecture makes it faster than it looks — build one owned spine first, then every channel integration is additive, not architectural.

Next Steps

What We Need to Start

Phase 1 can begin immediately without waiting on any third party. These are the decisions and inputs needed before the first line of code is written.

01

Hosting Decision

Medusa can be self-hosted (full ownership, ~€50-100/mo infra cost, you own everything) or via Medusa Cloud (managed, faster week-one start, monthly fee). Self-hosting is the right long-term answer for franchise scale. Cloud removes early friction. Which matters more right now?

Decide
02

Stripe Account Setup

Badedampfer needs a verified Stripe business account with VAT number and bank account. For the franchise model: a Stripe Connect platform account is required. Verification takes 1-5 days and cannot be rushed. Start this in parallel with week 1, not as an afterthought in week 5.

Start Now
03

Bókun Account Creation

Create a Bókun supplier account and begin listing products (boats, time-slots, pricing). OTA certification processes — especially GetYourGuide — start their clock from account creation date. Starting this in parallel with Phase 1 build saves 4-6 weeks of waiting.

Start Now
04

Product Inventory Definition

How many boats? What are the distinct product types — standard rental, premium, group? What are the time-slot windows, session durations, and maximum capacity per slot? This is the data model that every other layer builds on. Needs Jannis's input before anything else is modelled.

Jannis to Input
05

Franchise Architecture Scope

Is this build for Berlin only right now, with franchise support designed in but not activated? Or is Vienna a live requirement from day one? This affects the Stripe Connect setup and multi-tenant data model in Phase 1. Getting this wrong means refactoring the spine later.

Scope Decision
06

JSMD Supplier Registration

Jochen Schweizer and mydays operate through the JSMD Group supplier portal. Approval timelines can be 4-8 weeks. Register as early as possible so that Phase 4 is not blocked waiting on their approval while the rest of the platform is live.
