Etienne Frelechoz + Thomas MacFarlaine 45–60 min Biweekly English

Spotify Apple Podcasts

The thesis

AI is collapsing the distinction between GTM operators and decision-makers.

The operator who can't think strategically gets replaced. The executive who can't build gets outpaced.

Signal to Close is for the people becoming both: practitioners at the intersection of AI, revenue operations, and go-to-market execution.

The hosts

GTM Operator & Consultant

Etienne Frelechoz

B2B go-to-market operator specialising in AI-native GTM systems: sales, marketing, customer success, and revenue operations. European market focus.

On air: Drives GTM framing and operational use cases. Pushes for simplicity and what teams can realistically maintain.

AI Engineer

Thomas MacFarlaine

AI engineer with deep expertise in building intelligent systems and automation. Brings the architecture layer to every topic: how to actually build what GTM teams need, not just describe it.

On air: Leads technical architecture and tooling specifics. Pushes for building it right and challenges oversimplification.
The dynamic

Etienne pushes for operational simplicity and fast implementation. Thomas pushes for engineering correctness and scalable architecture. Disagreement is not smoothed over: it is the main event. Two complementary lenses on the same problem.

    Tactical

    How to Build a Pipeline That Actually Predicts Revenue

    Most pipelines are wishlists, not forecasts. A predictive pipeline is not more data. It is the right signals, measured at the right stages, with the right exit criteria. This episode is the build guide.

    “

    Your pipeline has $2.3M in it. Your CFO wants to know how much you’re going to close this quarter. You look at the number and you honestly have no idea. That’s not a forecasting problem. That’s a pipeline instrumentation problem.

  3. 003
    Contrarian

    AI Won’t Replace Your CRM. Here’s Why You Should Fix It First.

    The “CRM is dead” narrative is everywhere. This episode pushes back hard. AI doesn’t eliminate the need for a CRM; it makes clean, structured data more valuable than ever. Teams replacing their CRM with AI tools are solving the wrong problem.

    “

    I’ve heard three founders this month tell me they ditched their CRM for AI. Two of them had no idea what their win rate was. One couldn’t tell me their average deal cycle. That’s not AI-native. That’s flying blind with a chatbot.
